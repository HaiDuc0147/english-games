# Word Studio · Unit 4 Music & Arts

A one-player vocabulary game for the 20 words of Unit 4 (Music and Arts).

**Play:** https://haiduc0147.github.io/unit4-music-arts-game/

## Games
- 🧩 **Missing Letters**: fill the gaps in the word (`P_PP_T`).
- 🔀 **Scrambled Word**: put the mixed-up letters in order (`TRECNOC` → `CONCERT`).
- 🎲 **Mix Both**: a random game type for every word.

Each word shows a picture and the Vietnamese meaning as clues. After answering, the student hears the word and sees its IPA.

## How to play
- Tap letters (or type on a keyboard) to fill the boxes. Tap a filled box, or press ⌫, to take a letter back.
- A wrong answer sends only the wrong letters back. After 3 tries the answer is shown.
- 💡 Hint puts in one correct letter (−2 points). 🔊 plays the word.
- Points: 10 on the first try, 6 on the second, 3 on the third, +2 for a streak of 3 or more.
- **Easy**: fewer gaps, first letter of each word given in Scrambled. **Hard**: more gaps, 3 decoy letters, every letter scrambled.
- At the end, **Practise tricky words** replays only the words that were missed or needed help.
- 📖 **Study the words** shows all 20 cards with picture, IPA and meaning.

Best scores are saved on the device.

## Make a new word list
Copy this folder, edit `words.js`, and put a picture for each word in `images/`.

Pictures are clipart and photos from the web, used for classroom teaching only.
